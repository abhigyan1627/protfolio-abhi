FAQ

Common questions

Which documents do I need for filing my income tax return?

Typically PAN, Aadhaar, Form 16 (if salaried), bank statements, investment proofs and any other income details. Call us and we'll share an exact checklist for your case.

Do you help small shops and new businesses with GST?

Yes — from GST registration to regular return filing and replying to notices, we support shopkeepers, traders, startups and MSMEs.

Can I get help without visiting the office?

Yes. Many services can be handled over phone and WhatsApp with documents shared digitally. You're always welcome to visit the office in Kedia Lane, Gopalganj.

What are your fees?

Fees depend on the service and the complexity of your case. Clients consistently describe our services as affordable — call for a quick quote.

I received a notice from the tax department. What should I do?

Don't panic and don't ignore it. Share the notice with us as soon as possible so we can review it and respond within the deadline.
